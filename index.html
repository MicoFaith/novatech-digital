About NovaTech Digital Ltd

We are a public-interest technology company based in Kigali, Rwanda. We deliver specialized software development, digital solutions, and system integration on a contract basis for government institutions, businesses, NGOs, and individuals.

Rather than requiring organizations to build and maintain large in-house IT teams, we serve as a strategic technology partner — understanding the problem first, then designing and delivering practical, scalable solutions.
